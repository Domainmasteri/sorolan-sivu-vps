Privacy Policy for Sorolan JSON-muotoilija

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Introduction

Sorolan JSON-muotoilija ("the Tool") is a 100% local JSON formatter and validator committed to absolute user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Tool handles information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

The Tool runs entirely in your browser. All formatting and validation happens locally on your device — no data is ever sent to any server.

  • No Tracking: The Tool does not track your browsing history, monitor your behavior, or collect any analytics.
  • No Data Retention: The JSON data you enter is never saved, logged, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Fully Offline: The Tool works without an internet connection after the page has loaded.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or rent any data to third parties. The Tool contains no tracking codes, analytics, or advertisements, and it does not communicate with any external APIs or servers.

4. Security

Since all processing happens locally in your browser and no data leaves your device, your JSON data remains completely private and secure.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact us at: contact.make@sorola.fi
