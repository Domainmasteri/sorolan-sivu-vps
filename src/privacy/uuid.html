Privacy Policy for Sorola UUID Generator

1. Local Processing

The Sorola UUID/GUID Generator runs entirely locally in your browser using the Web Crypto API.

2. No Data Collection

No generated identifiers, device data, or personal information are collected, stored, or transmitted to any server. Your usage remains completely anonymous.

3. Security

Everything is processed in the browser's memory in real-time. The application contains no third-party tracking scripts.
