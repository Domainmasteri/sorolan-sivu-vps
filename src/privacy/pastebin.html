Privacy Policy for Sorolan Pastebin

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Introduction

Sorolan Pastebin ("the Service") is a text and code sharing service committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Service handles information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

When you create a paste, the text content you submit is stored on the server for as long as the paste is active. Pastes expire automatically according to the expiration time you set. After expiration, the content is deleted from the server.

  • No Account Required: No registration or personal identification is required to use the Service.
  • No Tracking: The Service does not track your browsing history, monitor your behavior, or collect personal analytics.
  • Content Responsibility: Do not store sensitive personal information, credentials, or private data in pastes, as pastes are accessible to anyone who has the link.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to any third parties. The Service does not contain third-party tracking codes or advertisements.

4. Security

All communication with the Service is conducted via secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols. However, paste content is not encrypted at rest — do not store sensitive or private information in pastes.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact us at: contact.make@sorola.fi
