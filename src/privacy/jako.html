Privacy Policy for Sorolan Tiedostojako

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Introduction

Sorolan Tiedostojako ("the Service") is a temporary file sharing service committed to protecting user privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Service handles your data.

2. Data Collection and Usage

When you upload a file, it is stored on the server for as long as the share is active. Files are automatically deleted when either the download limit is reached or the expiration time passes.

  • No Account Required: No registration or personal identification is required to use the Service.
  • No Tracking: The Service does not track your browsing history, monitor your behavior, or collect personal analytics.
  • Temporary Storage: Files are stored temporarily and deleted automatically once the share expires or the download limit is reached.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data or uploaded files to any third parties. The Service does not contain third-party tracking codes or advertisements.

4. Security

All communication with the Service is conducted via secure, encrypted HTTPS protocols. Files are accessible to anyone who has the share link. Do not share sensitive files without appropriate precautions.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, you can contact us at: contact.make@sorola.fi
